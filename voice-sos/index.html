Voice SOS

Call for help without touching your phone.

Every other safety app needs your hands. In the moment you are grabbed, running, or your phone is in your bag, that is exactly what you do not have. Voice SOS removes the button. You just use your voice.

How it works

Three things happen. You do one of them.

Pick a step to see it, or let it run. This is what the phone does, in order, from the word leaving your mouth to the people who can help knowing where you are.

  1. Say "help", "bachao" or "madad". SafeVaani is listening on the phone itself, with the screen off, the phone in a bag, and no signal at all. Nothing leaves the device to make that decision.

  2. One tap says you are fine. A pressed button gets 5 seconds. A voice trigger gets 3 to 9: shorter when the phone is being fought over and the word was shouted, longer when the phone is lying untouched. A whisper never makes you wait longer.

  3. Your circle gets your name and live location, whatever your sharing setting. SafeVaani members nearby are alerted. Recording starts, and keeps the 15 seconds before you spoke.

Why it stays private

It runs on your phone, not the cloud.

Most voice features send what the microphone hears to a server and wait for the answer. SafeVaani makes that decision on the phone itself. That is why it can keep listening in a lift, a basement or a highway with no signal, and why there is nothing to leak.

The one time audio does leave the phone is the moment you asked it to. When an SOS fires, the recording is saved to your private history, so what happened still exists afterwards. It is never sold, and never used for anything else.
