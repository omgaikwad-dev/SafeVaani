Creator questions, answered

Who counts as a person I brought in?+

Someone who joined with your code, verified their email, added an emergency contact and has at least one person in their circle. People who used your code but have not finished setting up show on your dashboard as still to count.

When and how do I get paid?+

You can ask for a payment once your balance reaches Rs. 100. It is sent to your UPI ID within 7 days. New earnings wait 7 days before you can withdraw them, so we can check everything is genuine.

Where do I see my numbers?+

Sign in at the creator dashboard with your email. We send you an 8 digit code, there is no password. It shows everyone counted, everyone still to count, your balance and every payment.

What if someone signs up just to game it?+

Sign-ups that turn out not to be genuine are reversed and do not count. Real people, set up properly, are what keeps somebody safe and what the program pays for.

Is this a job?+

No. Both are paid programmes, not employment. You post on your own schedule and can stop whenever you like.
