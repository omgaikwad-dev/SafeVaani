SafeVaani: The Voice SOS Safety App for Women in India

What SafeVaani Safety is and how its hands-free Voice SOS works. Say one word and your phone alerts your circle and nearby helpers with your live location, fully on-device and private.

If you have searched for an SafeVaani Safety SOS app, here is exactly what it is, how it works, and why it is built the way it is.

SafeVaani is a daily circle location and personal safety platform for India. It powers everyday group logistics, family tracking and friend check-ins, and it is a women’s safety app for India built around one idea: in a real emergency, you rarely have time to unlock your phone, find an app, and press a button. So SafeVaani lets you call for help with your voice. You say one word, and your phone does the rest.

What SafeVaani does

SafeVaani is a hands-free Voice SOS app. Set it up once, and from then on:

  • Say the word. SafeVaani listens for a panic word like “help, help”. When it hears you, it opens your SOS.
  • Your circle is alerted instantly. The people you trust get a notification with your live location, updated as you move.
  • Nearby helpers can respond. Verified helpers and other SafeVaani users near you can see that someone needs help and come to you, fast.
  • It works when you cannot touch the phone. With background protection on, SafeVaani can raise an SOS even with the screen locked or the app closed.

That is the whole point of a voice-activated SOS: help should arrive before panic does.

Why voice, not a panic button

A panic button sounds simple, but it fails in the exact moments that matter. Your phone is in your bag. Your hands are full. Someone is watching you. A button you cannot reach is a button that does nothing.

Your voice is always with you. Shouting “help” is the most natural thing a person does when they are scared, and SafeVaani turns that instinct into a real alert. You can read more about how this works on our Voice SOS page and the full features overview.

Private by design

A safety app that spies on you is not a safety app. So SafeVaani is built to keep your data yours:

  • Your voice never leaves your phone. The listening happens on-device. There is no cloud microphone.
  • We never sell your location or personal data. Not to advertisers, not to anyone.
  • You control your circle. You decide who can see you, and you can change it anytime.

You can read the details on our security page and privacy policy.

Use it on late commutes, night shifts and walks home

SafeVaani is for anyone who wants a faster, quieter way to call for help: students on late commutes, women working night shifts, anyone walking home alone, and families who want to stay connected to the people they love. It is designed for Indian streets and Indian networks, where an SOS needs to work even when the signal is weak.

Frequently asked questions

What is SafeVaani Safety?

SafeVaani is a women's safety app for India with a hands-free Voice SOS. You say one word and your phone quietly alerts the people in your circle, and nearby helpers, with your live location, even without unlocking the screen.

Is the SafeVaani SOS app free?

Yes. SafeVaani is free to start, and the core safety features, including Voice SOS and live location sharing with your circle, are free on the free plan. SafeVaani Plus is a paid tier that adds the ID-verified responder network.

How does the voice SOS actually work?

SafeVaani listens on your device for a panic word like 'help, help'. When it hears you, it opens the SOS and alerts your circle with your live location. The listening runs entirely on your phone, so it works even with a weak connection.

Does my voice or audio leave my phone?

No. The speech recognition runs on-device. Your audio is never uploaded to a server, so nobody, including SafeVaani, can listen to it.

Does SafeVaani work when the app is closed or the screen is locked?

Yes. With background protection turned on, SafeVaani can still hear your panic word and raise an SOS over the lock screen, so help is on the way before you can even reach for the phone.

Which phones does SafeVaani support?

SafeVaani is live on Google Play for Android and free to start. An iPhone version is planned but not yet available.
