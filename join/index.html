Careers at SafeVaani

Build the thing that
gets there in time.

SafeVaani is a voice-activated safety app for women in India. Say a word out loud and help is on the way, hands-free, offline, on a phone that is still in your pocket. We are early, bootstrapped, and looking for people who want that to exist.

How hiring works here

  1. 01

    You apply

    Ten minutes, no cover letter, no account to create.

  2. 02

    We read it

    A person reads every application. You hear back within a week, including if it is a no.

  3. 03

    A real conversation

    Forty-five minutes with Jatin. What you have built, what you want to build, what SafeVaani actually is.

  4. 04

    A paid work trial

    A small real problem, scoped to a few days, paid. Whiteboard puzzles tell us nothing about how you work.

Campus Ambassadors skip steps 3 and 4. You apply, we read it, and if it is a fit we send you everything you need to start. No interview, no work trial, and you can be running your first session inside a week.

At SafeVaani, we care for each other

Six things we can promise today. We are a two person company, so this list is short on purpose: everything on it is already true, and nothing on it is a plan.

The trial is paid

A few days of real, scoped work at a real rate. Nobody here proves they can work by working for free.

An answer inside a week

A person reads every application, and you hear back either way. Silence is not an answer we give.

No whiteboard puzzles

You will never be asked to invert a tree on a wall. We look at work you have done and work you do here.

A real equity conversation

We are early enough that this is a conversation and not a form. It happens with the people who stay.

Your work ships

There is no queue between you and the app. What you build this month is on a phone the month after.

The number comes first

We tell you what we can and cannot pay before you get invested, not after. It does not yet compete with a funded startup.

The honest part

We are early and bootstrapped. That means ownership, a founding-team title, real work in front of real users, and equity conversations for the people who stay. It does not yet mean a salary that competes with a funded startup, and we are not going to pretend otherwise on a page about honesty.

If you want a comfortable role, this is not it. If you want your work to be the reason someone got home, it is rare that you get to choose that.

If the mission pulls at you but none of the roles fit, say hello anyway. The worst that happens is a conversation.
