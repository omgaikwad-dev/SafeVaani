01

Get help hands-free

Two ways to raise an alarm today, and two more on the way. The first does not need your hands, and it is the reason SafeVaani exists.

The one to set up first

Voice SOS

Say the word. SafeVaani does the rest.

Say “help”, “bachao” or “madad” and the alarm goes out with the screen off, the phone in a bag and no signal. The word is recognised on the phone itself, so nothing you say is sent anywhere to be understood.

Where it listens
On your phone
Signal needed
None
Cancel window
3 to 9 seconds

“help”“bachao”“madad”
On the device. English built in, Hindi a one time download

Manual SOS

For the moments your hands are free. One press, a countdown you can cancel, and exactly the same alarm as Voice SOS.

Flat 5 second countdown
Next update

Scream detection

Turn it on and a clear scream on its own opens the SOS countdown, recognised on your phone like your trigger word. A TV or children playing can set it off, which is why the countdown is always there to cancel.

Next update

Silent shake SOS

Shake your phone hard about ten times in two seconds. One short buzz, a dark screen and a silent countdown, with no sound for anyone nearby to hear. Works while Voice SOS is on.

02

Reach the right people

An alarm is only as good as who hears it. These decide who that is.

Trusted circle

Add the people who look out for you. When an SOS fires they all get your name and live location at once, whatever your sharing setting is and whatever plan you are on.

Helpers in development

Nearby SafeVaani members

People near you who run SafeVaani are alerted too, so help is not limited to whoever is in your contacts. A verified responder network, SafeVaani Helpers, is being built on top of this.

One-tap 112

India’s national emergency line for police, ambulance and fire, one tap away from every SOS screen. SafeVaani supports that call. It never replaces it.

03

Stay protected on the move

For the stretches before anything has gone wrong, when all you want is for someone to know.

Safe Journey

Set where you are going and when you should arrive. If the time passes and you have not marked yourself safe, SafeVaani flags the journey overdue and moves straight to an SOS.

Walk with me

SafeVaani checks in out loud as you walk. Miss a check-in and a cancellable countdown starts, so a missed tap in a rickshaw is not a false alarm and a real silence is not ignored.

Plus

Safe zones

Draw a zone around home, college or work and hear when someone in your circle arrives at it or leaves it.

Check-in timer

Set a timer for a meeting, a cab or a late shift. If you do not check in before it runs out, SafeVaani raises the alarm for you.

04

Evidence and privacy

What happened should be on record. What you say and where you go should belong to you.

Automatic evidence

When an SOS fires, audio records automatically and keeps the 15 seconds before it, saved to your private history. Video recording during an SOS is part of Plus.

Next update

Incident report export

One tap turns an SOS into a timestamped PDF for a police complaint or an FIR: where it started, the location trail, recordings, who was alerted and who responded.

On-device by design

The word is recognised on your phone. The audio it listens to is never uploaded, streamed or stored on a server, and it works with no signal.

Never sold. Gone in 7 days.

No data brokers, no ad profiling, not to colleges, not to anyone. Location history is kept for 7 days so a route can be retraced, then deleted automatically.

Before you need it

Set it up tonight, before you need it.

Two minutes: pick your word, add the people who should hear it, and say it once to test. The first time you need it should not be the first time you use it.
