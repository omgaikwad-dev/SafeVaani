Trust

Security at SafeVaani

You trust us with the two most sensitive things you own: where you are, and your voice. We treat that as sacred.

Your voice stays on your phone

SafeVaani recognises your distress word using a model that runs on your own device. That listening audio is never uploaded, never streamed, and never stored anywhere, including on your phone. There is no ambient recording at any time.

Audio is kept only as evidence of an SOS. When you fire a real SOS, a short clip is uploaded to private storage only you can read, so that evidence survives a phone that was snatched or smashed. We delete it after 90 days and you can delete it sooner. That is the only audio we hold, and nothing is kept between emergencies.

Encryption

  • In transit: All data between the app and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • At rest: Data stored on our managed cloud infrastructure is encrypted at rest.

Strict access controls

We use row-level security so that each user can only ever access their own data. Sensitive uploads, such as any responder verification documents, are kept in private storage accessible only to authorised reviewers, never to other users.

Location is shared only when you choose

Your live location reaches your trusted circle during an active SOS, during a Safe Journey, and while you have Circle sharing switched on. Circle sharing is opt-in, you set how long it runs, and everyone in the circle can see who is currently visible, so it can never be used to watch someone secretly. It is unavailable on accounts belonging to under-18s.

Location history older than 7 days is permanently deleted by an automated job that runs nightly. We hold no long-term record of where you have been, and we never sell your location.

Honesty about limits

No system is perfectly secure, and we will never pretend otherwise on a safety product. We use strong, industry-standard protections and keep improving them. If a feature is not real or not ready, we do not claim it.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue in SafeVaani, please tell us responsibly at orbiisafety@gmail.com. Give us a reasonable chance to fix it before disclosing it publicly. We genuinely appreciate researchers who help keep users safe.
