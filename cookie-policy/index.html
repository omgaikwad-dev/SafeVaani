Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how SafeVaani Safety uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) on our website, safevaani.in. It applies to the website only. How the SafeVaani mobile app handles your data is covered in our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store on your device. Local storage is similar. Together they let a site remember things between visits, such as keeping you signed in.

What we use, and what we do not

We keep this simple and minimal:

  • Strictly necessary: If you sign in to a restricted area (such as the SafeVaani admin), we use secure cookies and local storage to keep you authenticated during your session. Without these, sign-in would not work.
  • Hosting essentials: Our hosting provider may set basic cookies required to serve the site securely and reliably.
  • No advertising cookies. We do not run ads and we do not use advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other websites.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site (such as signing in) from working, but browsing our public pages will still function.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The date above reflects the latest revision. Questions? Email us at orbiisafety@gmail.com.
