How do I get SafeVaani?

SafeVaani is live on the Google Play Store. Download it on any Android phone and set it up in a couple of minutes.

Download on Google Play →

How does Voice SOS work?

You set a distress word. When you say it, SafeVaani alerts your trusted circle with your live location, hands-free, even with the screen off. Detection happens on your device.

See how it works →

How do I set up my trusted circle?

Add the people you want alerted in an emergency inside the app. A circle holds up to four people. They receive your live location during an active SOS, during a Safe Journey, and while you have Circle sharing switched on.

Read the guide →

Is my data private?

Yes. Your distress word is recognised on your own phone and your audio is never uploaded. We never sell your location. See our Security page for details.
