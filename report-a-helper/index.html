This is taken seriously

Report a responder

If someone who came to help you behaved badly, tell us. You do not need the app, an account, or your name.

If you are in danger right now

Call 112. This page is read by a person, not a machine, and not instantly. It is not an emergency line.

What happens when you send this

  • A person reads it. If what you describe is credible, the responder's account is suspended before anything is investigated, because that takes seconds and can be undone, and waiting cannot.
  • We will not tell them who reported it. Ever.
  • You decide whether to go to the police. We do not decide that for you, and you can go whether or not you tell us. If you ask us to help, we will.
  • If you leave a way to contact you, someone will get in touch to check you are alright. If you do not, that is completely fine and the report still counts.

Tell us what happened

Only the first box is required. Leave anything else blank if you do not know it or would rather not say.

Are you safe now?

If you would rather email

orbiisafety@gmail.com. Write as much or as little as you like. It reaches the same person.

What we do and do not keep

A report is stored where no other user of SafeVaani can read it, including responders and including you once it is sent. Reports are kept for two years, so that a second report about the same person lands next to the first, and are deleted after that.

We never publish a report, never show it to the person it is about, and never share who sent it. See our Privacy Policy.
